Reproducible offline example, not a customer case or a purchase. These fixed demonstration dates use 24 October 2026 as the reference date. Replace dates before a new request. No private recipe texts are included.

1. The agency brief

A fictional local business wants six social media posts between 2 and 27 November 2026: two product posts, two team posts and two behind-the-scenes posts. Publishing on Monday, Wednesday or Friday; production preferably Tuesday or Thursday. No existing bookings are supplied.

A November publishing period can require production in October. The service only knows the dates you supply; it does not read your calendar or check people, holidays or actual availability.

3. A shoot is postponed

The client moves the first shared shoot from 29 October to 6 November. Send the complete posts_for_reschedule array from the plan as posts, plus this move object, to content-plan-shift. The downloadable JSON includes the complete example input.

{
  "post_key": "p1",
  "step": "shoot",
  "new_date": "2026-11-06",
  "shoot_mode": "group"
}

The date change affects p1, p2 and p3 together and returns seven changes. The second shoot group stays as it was.

Result: 1 error, 2 warnings, needs_confirmation: true. For p1, the new shoot is after its 2 November publication date. For p2, the shoot falls on its publication date. The service keeps those publication deadlines and flags the problems; it does not automatically extend every deadline.

Do not apply this change list blindly. Choose an earlier shoot or explicitly revise publication deadlines, then validate a new request. A new shift calculation is a separate purchase requiring its own approval. Weekends, holidays and shoot capacity are not checked by the shift service.
