    FAQ

    Do I need an account or an API key?

    No. Every request is paid individually, so there is no sign-up, no key and no subscription.

    What does it cost?

    Depending on the service {{MIN_SERVICE_PRICE}} to {{MAX_SERVICE_PRICE}} USDC per request; the "Local Market Check" package is {{LOCAL_MARKET_CHECK_PRICE}} USDC. 1 USDC is about 1 US dollar. You see the exact price before every payment.

    What is USDC and how do I get it?

    USDC is a digital dollar (stablecoin). Buy it for example in the Coinbase app and send it to your wallet on the "Base" network. You pay no network fees for payments to us.

    Which wallet do I need?

    A browser wallet that supports Base, for example Coinbase Wallet or MetaMask as an extension for Chrome, Firefox or Edge. The wallet shows amount and recipient before every payment and asks for your confirmation.

    Do I get an invoice?

    Yes, on request to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the request ID. Every payment can also be verified publicly.

    What if something goes wrong?

    You are never charged twice for the same payment. A failed paid request gets one free retry. Paid results can be fetched again with the result token. If anything stays unresolved, we refund on request.

    Where does the data come from and how fresh is it?

    The data comes live from DataForSEO, a large provider of search engine data. Google results are fetched live (identical requests are cached for a few hours at most); keyword and domain data come from regularly updated databases.

    What about data protection?

    The service is set up with privacy in mind: server in Germany, no IP addresses stored, inputs deleted after 30 days. Please do not enter personal data. Details in the privacy policy.

    May I use the data for my clients?

    Yes, for your own business purposes, including analyses, reports and proposals for your clients. Reselling the raw data as your own data product is not allowed. Details in the terms of use.

    Are there discounts for volume?

    Many services handle several items in one request, for example up to 1000 keywords or 10 domains. For larger volumes, just write to us.
